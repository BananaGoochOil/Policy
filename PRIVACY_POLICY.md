# Paper Trail Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Paper Trail** ("Paper Trail," the "Bot," "we," "us," or "our") collects, accesses, uses, stores, retains, and deletes information when Paper Trail is installed on or used within a Discord server.

Paper Trail is independently operated by its sole owner and developer in the United States. Paper Trail is not owned, operated, or controlled by Discord.

By using or interacting with Paper Trail, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. What Paper Trail Does

Paper Trail is a Discord moderation record and case-management application.

Its primary purpose is to allow authorized Discord server staff to maintain moderation records, user notes, case histories, audit records, exports, analytics, and related administrative information.

Paper Trail is designed to use information made available through Discord only to the extent reasonably necessary to provide this functionality.

## 2. Information Paper Trail May Access

Depending on the features used and the permissions granted to Paper Trail, the Bot may have access to information made available by Discord, including:

### Discord Server Information

- Discord server/guild ID;
- server name;
- channel IDs and information necessary to send Paper Trail messages;
- configured Paper Trail settings;
- roles and permissions relevant to Paper Trail's access controls; and
- information necessary to determine whether Paper Trail is installed and operating in a particular server.

### Discord User and Member Information

Paper Trail may access information necessary to identify users involved in moderation records, including:
- Discord user IDs;
- Discord moderator IDs;
- usernames or server nicknames when temporarily needed to identify or display a user during a Paper Trail operation;
- member information necessary for Paper Trail's functionality; and
- information associated with Discord interactions with Paper Trail.

Paper Trail does not intentionally maintain permanent nickname records as part of its case and note database.

### Moderation Information

Paper Trail may store information entered by authorized server staff, including:
- case numbers;
- moderation action types;
- reasons for moderation actions;
- notes;
- note entries and edit history;
- the Discord user associated with a record;
- the Discord moderator associated with a record;
- timestamps;
- deleted-record counters and related administrative information; and
- information required to operate Paper Trail's moderation and analytics features.

## 3. Discord Audit Log Information

Paper Trail may use Discord's audit-log functionality to automatically detect certain moderation actions performed through Discord.

For example, Paper Trail may automatically create case records for applicable kicks, bans, and timeouts.

Audit-log information is used solely to provide Paper Trail's moderation-record functionality and is not intentionally used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## 4. Message Content

Paper Trail does not intentionally use the Discord Message Content Intent as part of its current functionality.

Paper Trail is therefore not designed as a general-purpose message-monitoring or message-collection bot.

Paper Trail may nevertheless receive information directly associated with interactions users have with the Bot, such as information submitted through commands, forms, buttons, menus, or other Paper Trail features.

If Paper Trail's data-access model changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 5. Information Entered by Server Staff

Paper Trail allows authorized server staff to enter free-form moderation reasons and notes.

Because these fields are controlled by server staff, the developer cannot guarantee that users will not enter personal, sensitive, or otherwise inappropriate information into them.

Server administrators and authorized staff are responsible for using these fields appropriately and should avoid entering information that is unnecessary for the server's legitimate moderation purposes.

Paper Trail does not request or intentionally collect sensitive personal information for its own independent purposes.

## 6. How Paper Trail Uses Information

Paper Trail uses collected and accessed information only as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain its stated functionality.

This includes:
- creating and maintaining moderation records;
- identifying users and moderators associated with records;
- maintaining case and note histories;
- displaying moderation history to authorized staff;
- generating analytics and moderation summaries;
- generating requested data exports;
- posting configured moderation-log updates;
- enforcing Paper Trail's permissions and access controls;
- maintaining server-specific configuration;
- preventing abuse or unauthorized use;
- maintaining the security and reliability of Paper Trail;
- responding to support or technical issues; and
- complying with applicable law or valid legal obligations.

Paper Trail does not use Discord API Data for advertising, behavioral profiling, targeted marketing, or unrelated commercial purposes.

## 7. Data Ownership

Paper Trail treats moderation records created for a Discord server as that server's data.

The Paper Trail developer does not claim ownership of the underlying moderation records merely because Paper Trail processes or stores them.

The developer may process such information only as necessary to operate, maintain, secure, support, and comply with legal or platform requirements applicable to Paper Trail.

## 8. Data Sharing

Paper Trail does not sell, rent, license, monetize, or provide Discord API Data to advertisers, data brokers, marketing services, or other unrelated third parties.

Paper Trail does not intentionally share server or user data for advertising or commercial profiling.

The Paper Trail developer does not voluntarily disclose stored Paper Trail data to unrelated third parties except where necessary to provide Paper Trail's stated functionality or where otherwise permitted or required by applicable law or Discord requirements.

### Legal Requirements

Paper Trail may disclose information when the developer reasonably believes disclosure is legally required, including in response to a valid subpoena, court order, warrant, or other legally binding governmental or law-enforcement request.

Where legally permitted, the developer may seek to limit a request to information reasonably necessary to satisfy the legal obligation.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy prevents the developer from complying with applicable law.

## 9. Service Providers

Paper Trail is currently operated directly by the individual developer on their own computer.

Paper Trail does not currently use an external hosting provider or third-party data-processing service to operate its database.

If this changes, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 10. Data Storage and Security

Paper Trail's primary database is stored locally on the developer's computer rather than on a third-party cloud hosting service.

The developer takes reasonable measures intended to protect stored information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no computer system, database, network connection, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Paper Trail therefore does not guarantee that unauthorized access, data loss, or other security incidents can never occur.

## 11. Data Exports

Authorized server staff may request exports of Paper Trail records.

Exports may contain information including:
- Discord user IDs;
- Discord moderator IDs;
- case numbers;
- moderation action types;
- reasons and note text; and
- timestamps.

Paper Trail generates the requested export and delivers it to the requesting user through Discord as a private/hidden interaction where supported.

Paper Trail does not intentionally retain a separate copy of the generated export after delivery.

Once an export has been delivered, the Paper Trail developer does not control how the recipient stores, copies, transfers, or otherwise uses that file.

Recipients are responsible for protecting exported information.

## 12. Data Retention

Paper Trail retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its stated functionality, maintain moderation history, maintain security, comply with Discord requirements, resolve disputes, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Paper Trail does not use an arbitrary indefinite-retention policy for the purpose of accumulating user information.

Where data is no longer necessary for Paper Trail's permitted functionality and there is no legal or other permitted reason to retain it, it may be deleted.

### Server Removal

Removing Paper Trail from a Discord server does not necessarily cause the server's previously stored records to be immediately deleted.

Because moderation records may be necessary to preserve the server's historical moderation functionality, records may remain stored after the Bot leaves a server unless deletion is requested or deletion is otherwise required.

The developer will handle deletion requests in accordance with this Privacy Policy, Discord's requirements, and applicable law.

## 13. Account Availability and Automated Scrubbing

Paper Trail periodically checks stored Discord user IDs to determine whether the associated Discord account can still be retrieved.

If Discord indicates that an account can no longer be retrieved, Paper Trail may remove the moderation cases and notes associated with that account as part of its compliance and data-retention process.

Because Discord may not provide sufficient information for Paper Trail to determine the exact reason an account can no longer be retrieved, Paper Trail does not represent that every such event necessarily means the user deleted their account.

The purpose of this process is to avoid unnecessarily retaining moderation records associated with accounts that are no longer available through Discord.

## 14. Deletion Requests

Users and authorized server representatives may request deletion of Paper Trail data through the official Paper Trail Discord server:

https://discord.gg/jnXsqn9UM2

Requests should provide enough information for the developer to identify the relevant server, records, or user information.

The developer may request reasonable information necessary to verify that the requester is authorized to make the request.

Paper Trail will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period.

Deletion may be limited or denied where retaining particular information is necessary or legally required, including where retention is necessary to:
- comply with applicable law;
- comply with a valid legal or governmental request;
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims;
- investigate security incidents or abuse;
- comply with Discord requirements; or
- satisfy another legally permitted retention obligation.

Where only part of the requested information must be retained, the developer may delete the remaining information where reasonably practicable.

## 15. Server Administrator Responsibility

Discord server administrators decide whether Paper Trail is installed on their server and which staff members are authorized to access Paper Trail records.

Server administrators are responsible for:
- configuring Paper Trail's permissions appropriately;
- determining which staff members may access moderation records;
- ensuring that information entered into Paper Trail is appropriate;
- protecting Paper Trail exports;
- complying with applicable privacy and data-protection obligations applicable to their server or organization; and
- responding appropriately to requests from their members concerning moderation records where the server is responsible for those records.

Paper Trail does not independently determine whether a server's moderation practices comply with applicable law.

## 16. Discord's Role

Paper Trail is a third-party Discord application.

Discord independently controls its own services, systems, privacy practices, and policies.

Discord's applicable developer policies and terms govern Paper Trail's use of Discord and Discord API Data.

Users should also review Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

If Discord's requirements conflict with this Privacy Policy, Discord's applicable requirements will control to the extent of the conflict.

## 17. Children's Data

Paper Trail does not establish a separate age requirement from Discord.

Paper Trail is not intentionally directed toward children below the minimum age permitted by Discord or applicable law.

Paper Trail does not intentionally seek or collect information for the purpose of identifying children below applicable age requirements.

## 18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Paper Trail's functionality, data practices, Discord requirements, or applicable legal requirements change.

The current version will be made available through the official Paper Trail support or documentation channels.

Continued use of Paper Trail after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgment of the updated policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.
